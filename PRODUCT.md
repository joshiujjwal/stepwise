# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Individuals managing personal goals and tasks on mobile, often with executive-function challenges (including ADHD) and support needs around procrastination and productivity.

## Product Purpose
Stepwise helps users turn messy goals into small, time-boxed tasks they can execute and verify one step at a time. Success means users can reliably move from idea to completion with reduced overwhelm, clear next actions, and explicit done criteria.

## Brand Personality
Calm, practical, encouraging. The experience should feel steady and supportive under cognitive load, with clear structure and gentle momentum instead of pressure or noise.

## Anti-references
- Gamified or noisy productivity interfaces.
- Neon, “hacker” visual aesthetics.
- Dense enterprise-dashboard styling that overwhelms small-screen personal workflows.

## Design Principles
1. Reduce cognitive load by always foregrounding the next concrete action.
2. Keep progress honest with explicit acceptance criteria and clear state transitions.
3. Prefer calm clarity over stimulation: simple hierarchy, strong legibility, and minimal visual noise.
4. Support variable energy and attention with time-boxed choices and flexible execution paths.
5. Reinforce momentum through small wins, not gamification overload.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Default to high-contrast readable text, reduced-motion support, clear labels/instructions, generous tap targets, and plain language optimized for users under cognitive strain.
